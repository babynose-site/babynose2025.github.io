## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a gentle, clinically credible, and reassuring visual atmosphere tailored for pediatric respiratory health, infantile nasal hygiene, and bilingual/Persian (RTL) parental guidance. It bridges the authority of pediatric medicine with the nurturing warmth required for infant care.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Respiratory Clarity:** Visual space breathes freely with abundant padding, open airiness, and droplet-inspired geometry representing clear airways and natural relief.
- **Gentle Clinical Precision:** Medical credibility conveyed without sterile coldness. Soft structural framing, crisp editorial headlines, and reassuring tactile cards eliminate clinical anxiety for stressed caregivers.
- **Organic Fluidity & Micro-Glass:** Subtle atmospheric glass surfaces, layered aquatic gradients, and soft pill forms create an approachable sensory experience reminiscent of gentle mists, physiological saline, and pure water.
- **RTL & Typographic Harmony:** Built for native right-to-left flow (Persian/Farsi script paired with English numerals and Latin medical terms) using generous line heights and open letterforms.

## Colors

The palette balances refreshing respiratory mint tones with pediatric medical blues and deep slate clarity, ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility across text and functional controls.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Mint (`#58C9AD`):** Symbolizes effortless breathing, sterile freshness, and organic healing. Used for primary calls-to-action, success states, and key interactive highlights.
- **Primary Mint Soft (`#DDF5EC`):** Gentle wash for active card fills, respiratory indicator backgrounds, and positive health statuses.
- **Secondary Ocean Blue (`#278BCB`):** Represents clinical validation, pediatric authority, and saline solutions. Applied to headers, interactive links, medical badge borders, and primary navigation icons.
- **Secondary Air Blue (`#69C5E8`):** Atmospheric accent for droplet illustrations, progress meters, and dynamic state markers.
- **Aura Mist (`#EAF7FC`):** Soft luminous ground layer for floating modules, tooltips, and secondary card containers.
- **Deep Slate Navy (`#19384A`):** High-contrast, non-harsh text base delivering legible hierarchy and professional editorial gravitas.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F5F8FA`):** Cloud-soft baseline background tint preventing eye strain across prolonged reading and consultation flows.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level elevation containers, input surfaces, and crisp foreground cards.

### Semantic & Interaction Tones
- **Warning / Alert:** `#FF9F43` (gentle amber) for fever or dosage caution alerts.
- **Critical / Emergency:** `#EB5757` (coral-red) for pediatric emergency warnings and contraindications.
- **Disabled / Muted:** `#BAC7D0` with `#F0F4F8` surfaces.

## Typography

The typography pairing uses **Epilogue** for display, headlines, and milestones alongside **Quicksand** for accessible, friendly body reading and micro-labels. In localized Persian (Farsi) execution, these tokens map directly to calibrated geometric sans-serif equivalents (such as Vazirmatn) maintaining equivalent weights, generous vertical leading (+4px to +6px), and baseline optical balance.

### Typographic Principles
- **Airy Leading:** Nasal and pediatric instructions must be effortlessly legible in high-stress caregiver moments. Line heights never drop below 1.5x on instructional copy.
- **Bilingual Numerals & Metrics:** Milliliter dosages (`ml`), age ranges (`۰-۶ ماه`), and aspirator pressure steps are rendered with increased weight for rapid scanning.
- **Expressive Editorial Weight:** Epilogue provides character-rich structural anchors, avoiding clinical monotony while maintaining medical gravity.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid 12-column grid on desktop, an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column structure on mobile devices, calibrated for right-to-left layout inversion.

### Structural Flow & Gutters
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px), page margin `margin: 2.5rem` (40px) maxed at 1280px content width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1rem` (16px), page margin `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px), page margin `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px).
- **RTL Alignment Rules:** All horizontal margins, asymmetric paddings, back buttons, chevron badges, and icon-text pairings mirror horizontally (`margin-inline-start` / `margin-inline-end`).
- **Breathing Rhythm:** Sections within parental education portals and product display feeds utilize `space-3xl` between modules to emphasize sensory relief and unhurried browsing.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics liquid physics, light diffusion, and clean room sterile surfaces. Pure black shadows are strictly prohibited; all elevation models use deep cyan-slate hues (`#19384A`) at ultra-low opacity with generous diffusion.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F5F8FA` neutral floor. Elements blend directly into canvas or feature a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(39, 139, 203, 0.08)`).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Background `#FFFFFF`, shadow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(25, 56, 74, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(25, 56, 74, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Hover):** Shadow: `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(25, 56, 74, 0.07), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(39, 139, 203, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Cart Drawers / Dosage Overlays):** Shadow: `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(25, 56, 74, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(88, 201, 173, 0.08)`.

### Glassmorphism & Mist Layers
- **Droplet Glass Badges:** Used for clinical badges, saline sterile tags, and stock urgency. Surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`, backdrop filter `blur(12px) saturate(160%)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
- **Respiratory Mist Float:** Header bars and floating navigation capsules use `rgba(245, 248, 250, 0.85)` with `blur(16px)` to integrate softly over educational imagery.

## Shapes

With pill-shaped roundedness (`3`), the interface eliminates harsh spikes and threatening geometry, reflecting the physical safety of silicone nasal aspirator tips, baby-safe soft edges, and gentle droplet contours.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro Tokens (`rounded-base`):** `1rem` (16px) for interactive buttons, badge chips, input fields, and pill selectors.
- **Macro Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `2rem` (32px) for product cards, dosage calculators, and consultation modules.
- **Asymmetric Droplet Accent (`rounded-xl`):** `3rem` (48px) on featured instructional hero modules, hero imagery frames, and educational callout banners. Select graphic containers employ an organic asymmetrical droplet curve: `border-radius: 3rem 3rem 3rem 1rem` (mirrored in RTL to `3rem 3rem 1rem 3rem`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Nasal Mist Green):** Background `#58C9AD`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded-full` (height 48px or 56px for key conversion steps). Micro-interaction: subtle scale (`0.98`) and upward translation on hover with a soft mint glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(88, 201, 173, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action (Ocean Blue Tint):** Background `#EAF7FC`, text `#278BCB`, border `1px solid rgba(39, 139, 203, 0.2)`, radius `rounded-full`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, text `#19384A`, hover state `#DDF5EC` at 50% opacity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Clinical Pediatrist Verification Chip:** Pill container with a mini medical shield or droplet icon. Surface `rgba(221, 245, 236, 0.9)`, border `1px solid #58C9AD`, text `#19384A`, font `label-md`.
- **Age-Appropriate Indicator (e.g., +0 Months / نوزادی):** Soft blue glass base (`rgba(234, 247, 252, 0.8)`), text `#278BCB`, radius `rounded-full`, padding `4px 12px`.

### Cards & Product Modules
- **E-Commerce Product Card:** Clean white baseline container with `rounded-2xl` (32px) corners, `space-lg` inner padding. Features an upper droplet-badge for medical certification, product photography on a soft `#F5F8FA` circular bed, clear Persian typography, and an icon-only or pill-shaped add-to-cart action.
- **Parental Guide Educational Article Card:** Split layout featuring soft rounded visual media on one side, title in Epilogue/Vazirmatn bold, estimated reading duration in `label-sm`, and key symptom tags.

### Inputs & Dosage Selectors
- **Form Text Fields:** Height 52px, radius `1rem`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#19384A`. Active focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(88, 201, 173, 0.25)` and border color `#58C9AD`. RTL text alignment by default with placeholder in `#BAC7D0`.
- **Age & Suction-Strength Radio Pills:** Connected pill selector with animated sliding capsule background in `#278BCB`, delivering tactile selection clarity.

### Specialized Respiratory Components
- **Saline Dosage & Spray Frequency Meter:** Segmented visual bar with droplet-shaped step indicators transitioning from `#DDF5EC` to `#58C9AD`.
- **Mucus Color & Health Guide Selector:** Soft touch cards comparing nasal secretion textures with doctor-vetted advisory tags and immediate triage cues.
- **RTL Navigation Bar:** Fixed or floating pill navigation with mirrored back buttons, cart badge on top-left, and quick pediatric helpline trigger in primary mint on top-right.
